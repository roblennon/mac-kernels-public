# Lessons for card writers and runners

Read this before writing or revising a card. The card shape that follows from it is [designs/TEMPLATE.md](designs/TEMPLATE.md). The dated record stays in [the checkpoint](checkpoint-2026-11-09.md).

**Gate the subject before testing the hypothesis.** Before the step that tests the hypothesis, confirm that the model, task, instrument or data has the property the test depends on, with room for the effect to show, against a bar written in advance, with a stop. A planted-effect check does not count, because it tests the instrument, not the subject. Copies-as-evidence step 1a passed every measuring check in the lab's case 002 and still decided nothing: with thinking off, the 1.7B model got no greedy answer right, so the hypothesis was never tested. Evidence: "Result of step 1a" in [copies-as-evidence](designs/copies-as-evidence.md); the checkpoint's "What was learned"; commit 6d884fd, which added gates to the other queued cards.

**Reviewers check what the artifact names, so the review prompt must name the subject question.** Four review passes and four cold reads of copies-as-evidence checked the measurement and never asked whether the model could do the base task. The fix is the question, not more reviewers. Every attack and cold-read prompt asks it outright. Evidence: the copies-as-evidence review record; the lab's process log.

**Set tolerances above the format's own error.** Derive the error the numeric format makes by itself (bf16 rounding, accumulation order) first, then set each check's tolerance above it. Copies-as-evidence's first identity stop of 0.1 nat sat below the card's own bf16 bound, and the library reference later failed on bf16 rounding at p near 0.5. Evidence: the copies-as-evidence review record; commits 8952600 and 57e40c3.

**A reading has one source.** The card's result section for that run is the only place a reading of it lives. Messages point to it rather than restate numbers, and a correction lists every place the old reading went. The example: the reading that the model was "at chance" on the sum (−2.4 nats per digit against −2.3) averaged digits that behave differently. In fact one template was inaccurate and the other broke format. Before the card was corrected, the phrase had reached the lab's case 002 outcome record (lab commit 3af165f), the lesson text given to about 30 sweep agents, and the review records of about 22 cards. Every copy in this repository was corrected on 2026-11-10; the lab's three copies are in its own cleanup list. Evidence: "Result of step 1a" in [copies-as-evidence](designs/copies-as-evidence.md).

**A card holds the current design, not its history.** When a review round changes a card, rewrite the affected text in place and add one dated line of at most two sentences to the review record. Copies-as-evidence reached 15,176 words, with a "Step 1, exactly" of about 9,000 and review entries of 300 to 500 words each, because every round appended and none removed. Evidence: `wc -w designs/*.md`; the copies-as-evidence review record.

**A threshold changed after its check fails is a plan change.** Record the failing values, and have the lab's skeptic look before the plan is rebound to the new card commit. The p 0.9 floor for the library reference was proposed after that check failed in the second pilot and accepted the same minute (13:35 UTC). Evidence: commit 57e40c3.

**Search fan-out runs three agents at a time, against a budget of about 200 calls per lead turn.** Web search is shared across every subagent of a lead turn. About 30 search agents launched at once drained it within minutes, twice in one session, and most cards' first search gate had to be redone. Evidence: the checkpoint's "What was learned"; the decision in [TASKS.md](TASKS.md); Slice A in [FRAME.md](FRAME.md).

**Cross-family review earns its place at queue and design level.** A reviewer from another model family read the queue and its top cards after four Claude passes. It found a wrong confidence bound (no events in N draws bounds p at about 3/N, not 1/N²), an identification argument in img-subliminal that could not separate its two claims, and a queue top that repeated one operational bet. Keep it there. It did not catch the subject gap either, which is the lesson above, not a reason to add passes. Evidence: [the queue review](reviews/2026-11-09-queue-review-sol.md); the checkpoint's "What was learned".
